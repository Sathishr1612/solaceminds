Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you wanted to ask

Still have a question?Chat with us on WhatsApp
What services does Solace Minds offer?

We provide Life Coaching, Kids Counselling, Group Counselling, Marital Counselling, Emotional Support, Motivation Sessions, Mental Wellness Workshops, and Awareness Programs for individuals, families, educational institutions, and organizations.

How do I know which service is right for me?

During your initial consultation, we take time to understand your concerns, goals, and expectations before recommending the most suitable counselling or coaching approach.

Are all counselling sessions confidential?

Yes. Confidentiality is one of our core values. Every session is conducted with professionalism, empathy, and respect for your privacy.

Can I book an appointment online?

Yes. You can schedule an appointment through our website, or contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or email.

How long does a counselling session usually last?

Most sessions last between 45 and 60 minutes, depending on the type of service and individual requirements.

How many sessions will I need?

There is no fixed number. Some individuals benefit from a few sessions, while others may choose longer-term support based on their personal goals and progress.

Do you provide counselling for children?

Yes. Our Kids Counselling services are designed to support children's emotional, behavioural, academic, and social development in a safe and nurturing environment.

Do you conduct workshops for schools and colleges?

Absolutely. We regularly conduct mental wellness programs, awareness sessions, motivation talks, and emotional well-being workshops for schools, colleges, and educational institutions.

Are corporate wellness programs available?

Yes. Solace Minds offers customized corporate wellness programs, employee motivation sessions, stress management workshops, leadership development sessions, and mental wellness initiatives for organizations.

Is seeking counselling a sign of weakness?

Not at all. Seeking support demonstrates self-awareness and a willingness to grow. Reaching out for help is a positive step toward better mental health, stronger relationships, and improved overall well-being.
