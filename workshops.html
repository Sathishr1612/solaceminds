Awareness Programs & Workshops

Inspiring minds. Building resilient communities.

Creating awareness & promoting mental well-being.

At Solace Minds, we conduct interactive mental wellness workshops for schools, colleges, corporates, NGOs, and community groups. Why Mental Wellness Awareness Matters

Awareness today, healthier communities tomorrow

Mental wellness is a shared responsibility. Creating awareness is the first step toward healthier individuals, stronger families, productive workplaces, and emotionally resilient communities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Planning a workshop?

Still have a question?Chat with us on WhatsApp
Can workshops be customized?

Yes. Every program is designed based on your audience, objectives, and organization.

Do you conduct online workshops?

Yes. Depending on your requirements, workshops can be conducted online, offline, or in a hybrid format.

What is the ideal group size?

Programs can be conducted for small teams, classrooms, or large audiences. The format is adapted to ensure meaningful participation.

How long are the workshops?

Workshops typically range from 60 minutes to a full day, depending on the topic and learning objectives.

How can we book a workshop?

Simply complete the enquiry form or contact us via phone, email, or WhatsApp. Our team will discuss your requirements and prepare a customized program.
