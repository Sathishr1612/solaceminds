Our Story

Supporting every step toward better mental wellness

Solace Minds was founded with a simple purpose: to create a safe, supportive, and judgment-free space where individuals can heal, grow, and thrive.

We believe everyone deserves compassionate guidance to navigate life's emotional challenges with confidence.

Through professional counselling, life coaching, and mental wellness programs, we empower individuals, families, students, and organizations to build emotional resilience, healthier relationships, and lasting well-being.

Our Mission

Empowering minds through compassionate care

To provide accessible, ethical, and confidential mental wellness support that promotes emotional well-being, reduces stigma, and equips individuals with practical tools for lifelong resilience and balanced living.

Our Vision

Creating a mentally healthier society

To build a future where mental wellness is valued as much as physical health, empowering individuals, families, schools, and organizations through trusted counselling, life coaching, and holistic wellness services.

Meet Mrs. Sowmya Basavaraj

Founder & Mental Wellness Professional

Every conversation has the power to change a life.

Mrs. Sowmya Basavaraj founded Solace Minds with a deep passion for helping people navigate life's emotional challenges with confidence, resilience, and hope.

Throughout her journey, she has witnessed how stress, anxiety, relationship conflicts, academic pressure, parenting concerns, and emotional struggles can affect individuals and families. These experiences strengthened her belief that everyone deserves access to compassionate guidance and professional emotional support.

Her approach combines active listening, empathy, practical problem-solving, and evidence-based counselling techniques to help individuals gain clarity, improve emotional well-being, and achieve personal growth.

With every counselling session, workshop, and motivational program, Mrs. Sowmya strives to create a safe and encouraging environment where people feel understood, accepted, and empowered to move forward.

She believes that healing begins when individuals feel heard, and that every step toward seeking help is a courageous step toward a healthier and happier life.

Our Approach to Mental Wellness

Personalized. Holistic. Client-centered.

No two individuals experience life in the same way. That's why our counselling approach is tailored to the unique needs, goals, and circumstances of every client.

  1. 01

    We Begin by Listening

    Every journey starts with understanding your story, concerns, emotions, and aspirations.

  2. 02
